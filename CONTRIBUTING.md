# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the Digital Lean Framework.

DLF is an authored framework distributed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. To keep a single, coherent canonical text, **the framework is written and maintained by the author only. Pull requests are not accepted.**

Your experience is still the most valuable input the framework can get. Please use [Issues](../../issues/new/choose) to:

- **Share a field report**: you applied DLF (an assessment, a muda scan, a Sprint Kaizen, a Law Zero decision). What worked, what did not, what was missing?
- **Report an erratum**: a broken cross-reference, an inconsistent formula, a typo.
- **Ask a question** about how a part of the framework is meant to be applied.

Please do not include confidential information about your organization, clients or colleagues in issues: they are public.

Ideas that make it into the framework are acknowledged in the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) unless you prefer otherwise.
