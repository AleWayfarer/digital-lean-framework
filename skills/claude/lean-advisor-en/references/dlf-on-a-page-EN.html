SECTION 6
Lean Test — three anti-theater questions (end of sprint)
TEST 1 — TOOL
Orphan tool
For every tool you used in this sprint, can you name in 30s a) which DLF principle it serves and b) what value the customer gets from it?
No → orphan tool. Suspend it or reconnect to its principle.
TEST 2 — METHOD
Cosmetic method
In the last 3 sprints, did a flow metric actually move? DLI, flow ratio, throughput, matrix position, traction work?
No → cosmetic method. Diagnose source and dimension.
TEST 3 — STRATEGY
Strategy adrift
In the last 3 sprints, did any customer (internal or external) get a real gain? Fewer reminders, fewer escalations?
No → strategy adrift. Go back to Step 0A/0C.
Three yes = framework aligned · One no = fix it · Two no = stop · Three no = back to Law Zero
